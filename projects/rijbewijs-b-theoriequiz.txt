## What I built
A single-page quiz app covering the full Belgian driving theory curriculum. It scrapes the chapter structure from gratisrijbewijsonline.be, which publishes 32 PDF study documents, and turns that into 300 questions across 9 chapters — road types, speed limits, right of way, parking, alcohol rules, vehicle tech, and more. You can filter by chapter, get instant feedback with an explanation after every answer, and see a results screen with a score ring at the end.

## Tech stack
- **HTML/CSS/JS** — no frameworks, everything in a single self-contained file
- **CSS custom properties** for dark-themed design and consistent styling
- **Vanilla JS** for quiz state, chapter filtering, score tracking and shuffle logic

## What I learned
Writing 300 questions by hand — each with three plausible wrong answers and a clear explanation — gives you a real appreciation for exam question design. On the code side it was a good exercise in keeping plain JS clean without reaching for a framework: one shared state object, no dependencies, fully portable HTML file.
